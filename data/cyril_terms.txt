====================================
PATRISTIC TERMINOLOGY DATASET: CYRIL OF ALEXANDRIA & THEODORET OF CYRUS
====================================
[ENTRY_01]
LEMMA: προχέω (procheō)
FORMS_FOUND: προχεόμενον (participle), προχεῖται (middle/passive 3rd sing)
MEANING: To pour forth, flow forth
THEOLOGICAL_DOMAIN: Essential communication / Homoousion (not hypostatic origin)
LOCUS: De adoratione in spiritu et veritate 1 (PG 68:148)
GREEK_TEXT: εἴ περ ἐστὶ τοῦ Θεοῦ καὶ Πατρὸς καὶ μὴν καὶ τοῦ Υἱοῦ τὸ οὐσιωδῶς ἐξ ἀμφοῖν ἤγουν ἐκ Πατρὸς δι' Υἱοῦ προχεόμενον Πνεῦμα
ENGLISH: "The Spirit of God the Father and the Son also, Which is poured forth ESSENTIALLY from Both, i.e., from the Father through the Son."
PREPOSITION_USED: διά (through the Son)

[ENTRY_02]
LEMMA: ἐκπορεύεσθαι (ekporeuesthai) / ἐκπόρευσις (ekporeusis)
FORMS_FOUND: ἐκπορεύεται (pres. ind. 3rd sing), ἐκπορευόμενον (participle)
MEANING: To proceed forth from
THEOLOGICAL_DOMAIN: Hypostatic origin / Personal existence from ultimate cause (aitia)
LOCUS: Gospel of John 15:26 / Symbolum Constantinopolitanum / Cyril's Apologia
GREEK_TEXT: τὸ ἐκ τοῦ Πατρὸς ἐκπορευόμενον
RULE: Cyril strictly reserves this verb for the Spirit's origin from the Father alone as arche/aitia.
PREPOSITION_USED: ἐκ (from the Father)

[ENTRY_03]
LEMMA: προϊέναι (proienai) / πρόεισι (proeisi)
MEANING: To go forth, advance
THEOLOGICAL_DOMAIN: Relaxed usage; covers essential relation, indwelling, and economic mission
RULE: Used flexibly by Cyril without implying the Son is a co-cause (aitia) of hypostatic existence.

[ENTRY_04]
CONTROVERSY: Theodoret of Cyrus - Reprehensio duodecim anathematismorum
TARGET: Cyril's 9th Anathema (ἴδιον τοῦ Χριστοῦ τὸ Πνεῦμα)
CONTESTED_FORMULA: ἐξ Υἱοῦ ἢ δι' Υἱοῦ τὴν ὕπαρξιν ἔχον ("having its origin/existence out of or through the Son")
STATUS: Rejected by Theodoret as blasphemous if taken hypostatically; reconciled in 433 Formula of Reunion when Cyril confirmed the Spirit derives hypostatic existence from the Father alone.
