# Cyril of Alexandria and the Greek Language of the Holy Spirit

## προχέω (procheō)

The term προχέω means "to pour forth" or "flow forth."

Cyril of Alexandria uses this terminology when describing the Holy Spirit's relation to the Father and the Son.

## ἐκπόρευσις (ekporeusis)

This term refers specifically to procession from the Father as source.

## Theodoret of Cyrus

Theodoret questioned Cyril's language because he was concerned that "through the Son" might imply another source of existence.

After clarification, the distinction between essential relationship and causal origin became clearer.
